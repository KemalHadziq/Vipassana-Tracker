Smartwatch alerts

Connect a Bluetooth heart rate source. If your heart rate stays well above your usual level, you get an alert and a short session is suggested. Rating your stress 7 or higher before a session also triggers the alert.

Needs Chrome or Edge with Web Bluetooth (Android or desktop, not iPhone). Many watches share heart rate over Bluetooth only when a broadcast heart rate mode is switched on, and some do not support it at all. Alerts reach your watch through phone notification mirroring, so allow notifications in the reminders card. Heart rate also rises with movement or caffeine, so treat an alert as a prompt, not a diagnosis.

The three phases

Anapana. Rest your attention on the feeling of breath at the nostrils. It steadies the mind.

Vipassana. Move attention through sensations across the body and accept each one without reacting.

Metta. Close with goodwill and compassion toward all beings.

The default 10, 15 and 5 minute split follows the method of S. N. Goenka, as used in the study.

What the research found

Delgado-Pastor and colleagues (2013) tested ten experienced Vipassana meditators on two days: one with a 30-minute meditation and one with 30 minutes of random thinking. After meditating, the meditators showed a larger P3b brain response to a target tone, which the authors read as greater attentional engagement. During the Vipassana phase, their LF/HF heart rate ratio also rose more than during random thinking.

The authors call this suggestive evidence. The group was small, there was no non-meditator comparison, and the heart result was only marginally significant. This app records your own ratings and cannot measure your brain or heart, and it is not a medical tool. If stress or alcohol use feels hard to manage alone, a doctor or counsellor can support you alongside meditation.
